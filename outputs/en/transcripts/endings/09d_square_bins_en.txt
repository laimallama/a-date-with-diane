Diane Pees Behind the Bins

Diane has disappeared, urgently seeking a toilet. You want to see how she gets on.

Casting all discretion to the winds, and chancing your luck, you head off in search of her.

You leave the bus stop.

(Where did she go?)

Which way did she go? You look around and will have to guess. Which will it be?

(Down the subway beneath the road, which will lead her back into town?)

You head down the subway. It's dank and smells of urine.

You walk through the subway. You can hear footsteps disappearing into the distance ahead of you – it must be Diane.

The subway divides into two at the other end, with steps to the left and a passageway to the right.

Which way should you go?

(Along the passage?)

You go along the passage. You can hear footsteps ahead of you.

You follow cautiously towards the end of the passage. The footsteps seem to come to a halt.

(You pause, then move forward slowly.)

The passage emerges into a small square or piazza. You peep cautiously round the corner. You recognise it – during the day there are often market stalls here. But at this time of night it's deserted.

Suddenly you hear footsteps again. It's Diane, hurrying back across the square, about 15 yards away from you. In her dark skirt she almost blends into the darkness, but her blonde hair catches the light. A couple of wheelie bins have been pushed to one wall and she walks over to them.

She stops and looks round, bending forward, a hand between her legs.

She looks round again, then you get a flash of white petticoat as she starts to hitch up her skirt.

(You step back into the shadows.)

Diane moves two or three yards, behind the wheelie-bins. She is hidden from the street, but you have a perfect view. You just wish it wasn't so dark.

She fiddles with her tights but quickly pulls them and her knickers down and squats, with one final anxious look around.

You can hear the faint splash of her pee as she finally gets to relieve herself – and within a few seconds a puddle, glistening in the moonlight, is running across the concrete away from her.

She pees for perhaps half a minute. Then you watch as she stands up, pulls up her knickers and tights, tidies her skirt, then hurries away across the square.
