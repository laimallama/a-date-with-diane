Giving Diane the Brooch

You can tell that Diane is dying for a pee. She keeps fidgeting about. But somehow she seems shy about asking.

But you've decided to be the perfect host and help her. What will it be?

(Choose this moment to give her the brooch.)

YOU: I'm just going to get something.

You go across the room to fetch the brooch. Diane stands up.

DIANE: I must be going. I'll just – .

YOU: (interrupting her) I've got something for you.

You return and give it to her. She has sat down on the sofa again and is putting on her jacket.

DIANE: Oh, it's lovely! Thank you.

She gives you a kiss and is about to say something else but –

You gained 15 intimacy points.

YOU: I'll walk you home. We'll creep out quietly so we don't wake my brother.

You open the door for her.

(You get ready to walk her home.)

You head towards the front door.

You can hear footsteps in the room above. That will be your brother. So there's nobody in the bathroom.

You're not sure whether Diane realises that. Maybe she does, maybe she doesn't.

Are you going to tell her?
