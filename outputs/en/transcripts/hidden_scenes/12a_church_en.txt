The Bus Ride Home: Diane Pees by the Church

As you zip yourself up again, you hear something jingle in your jacket pocket.

It's the brooch! You'd completely forgotten about it.

Maybe if you hurried back you might catch her before she gets home.

(It's worth a try.)

You run back towards Diane's bus stop. You might be in time to catch her before she gets home.

But you can't see her anywhere.

You stop and look around.

(But there's no sign of her.)

You presume she must have hurried home. So you turn for home yourself.

But just as you do so, something catches your eye.

There is a flash of white across the road, in the lych-gate of a church. And you can see that there, in the half-light, there is a girl having a pee.

Even as you watch from the shadows, she stands up, her black skirt and white petticoat hitched around her waist as she pulls up her knickers.

Then, looking nervously around, she pulls up and straightens her tights. She lets her skirt fall into place but then half hitches it up again while she straightens her tights.

And off she goes, luckily without noticing you.

Your mind races. After all, she only had about 200 yards to walk home from the bus stop, so she must have been desperate enough that she couldn't make it. She must have lost precious minutes hunting for cover, or waiting for the street to clear, and only then could she finally relieve herself. Did she get her knickers down in time?

Maybe one day you'll ask her, but for now –

(The game's over.)
