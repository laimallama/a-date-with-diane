Outdoor Endings – You and the Taxi Driver Watch Diane

The taxi is getting near to where you live. It will arrive at her house first, but yours is only a couple of streets away.

YOU: Would you like to come in for a coffee?

DIANE: Thanks, but it's late and I'm quite tired. It's been a lovely evening and I'll see you next week. If you don't mind, I'll just get out now.

You guess that the real reason is that she's bursting for the loo. But there doesn't seem any way you can follow.

The taxi draws up. Diane gives you a kiss and gets out.

For a moment you see her shifting from foot to foot as she searches for her key, then the taxi moves out of sight of the door.

(It's been a nice evening, but –)

The taxi draws away. You glimpse her out of the rear window. She goes to the front door.

She's trying to put the key in the door, but she can't stand still.

She stands on one leg, then she stands on the other. She's having problems with the key.

The taxi has to turn round, but Diane comes into view again.

You glimpse her still fiddling with her key, while shifting from foot to foot.

Oh! She seems to have dropped the keys.

You wish you could watch her.

(Maybe I'll play a luckshot.)

You can tell Diane is desperate for a pee. You'd like to see how she gets on – so you've played a luckshot.

You don't know what will happen. But there's nothing to lose, is there?

(Wait and see.)

The taxi finishes turning round.

The taxi driver chuckles. 'D'you want to watch?' he says.

You're taken by surprise. 'Er, yes,' you reply.

'Always good manners to check that a girl is safely home,' he says. He chuckles again. 'Especially when they're busting for a piss.'

He turns off the engine.

(Wait and see.)

The two of you watch in silence.

Diane has picked up the keys and is trying the lock again. She can't stand still, but is walking on the spot even as she fiddles with the key.

She turns away from the door to look at the key in better light. She is holding herself.

She walks around in a little circle, one hand firmly at her crotch. Then tries the key again.

She rings the bell. Nobody answers.

She paces about. Then sits down on the doorstep to search in her handbag.

She takes out another key and starts to try the lock again – but straight away breaks away and shoves a hand to her crotch.

She walks round in an agitated little circle, holding herself all the time, then tries the lock again.

She turns away from the door again, holding the hem of her skirt as if about to hitch it up, then disappears from view as if going to the back of the house.

But within a few seconds she is back. She can't stand still but tries the lock once more.

(You hold your breath.)

She hasn't seen you. It's dark where you are in the taxi, but you can see her in the lights outside the house.

She does a little knees-up dance, then a curtsey and shoves both hands to her crotch. You imagine a squirt of pee dribbling down her leg.

She goes back to the door. She tries the key again. She crosses one leg tightly over the other.

She sits on the top step, hunched forward, bouncing up and down. Her hand looks as if it's right under her skirt.

She gets up, paces about, then jigs round in a little circle.

(And then…)

Suddenly she stops in her tracks. She bends slightly forward. Then looks anxiously around.

There seem to be a few drops of rain by her feet. Is it your imagination?

The few drops have become a small puddle. The puddle grows and begins to dribble in a little stream across the paving.

You imagine her pee trickling down her tights.

One hand is still at her crotch, the other covers her face, as if trying to hide her shame from herself.

Her legs are glistening in the moonlight.

Eventually she is finished. Diane slowly walks away from the puddle, takes out a tissue and starts wiping her legs.

She sits on the top doorstep for a minute, as if to collect her thoughts. Then she goes to the door. This time she manages to unlock it and slowly goes in.

(Gosh!)

'Phew!' says the taxi driver. Neither of you speaks until he starts driving away.

'I see a few desperate girls in this job,' he says. 'But that's one of the best.'

'I reckon this taxi ride will be free,' he says, and takes you the rest of the way home.

(Thank you!)

CONGRATULATIONS!

You've taken Diane out on a date and enjoyed watching her get desperate for a pee.

And you've seen her wet herself on her front doorstep.

But you've had to share your sighting with the taxi driver – who had the better view. So –

YOU'VE ONLY WON A CONSOLATION PRIZE!
