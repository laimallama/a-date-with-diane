Saturday Car Park Route

Diane has disappeared, urgently seeking a toilet. You want to see how she gets on.

Casting all discretion to the winds, and chancing your luck, you head off in search of her.

You leave the bus stop.

(Where did she go?)

Which way did she go? You look around and will have to guess. Which will it be?

(Into the car park? There's a public toilet on the other side, or maybe she'll squat behind a car.)

You head into the car park. You look left, you look right. There's no sign of Diane.

You see a large camper van. Maybe she's popped behind that. You check it out – she isn't there.

You walk across the car park. You know there are toilets on the far side but they are sure to be closed at this time.

Suddenly you hear footsteps, the sound of a girl running.

It's Diane. She must have gone to the side of the car park but there are people there and nowhere discreet enough to squat. You watch her hurry as best she can towards the toilets.

She goes up to the door marked Ladies, but it is locked. She tries the door but it won't open.

You step back into the shadows.

(What will she do?)

Diane steps away from the toilet. She walks this way, then that way, she stands and looks into the car park. She is holding her bag with one hand but the other is deep in her dress pocket. She's definitely holding herself.

She hurries behind a car and you see her begin to hitch her dress up. But the sound of a group of approaching drunks frightens her away. She walks towards you, is only about 20 yards away, then suddenly stops and shoves a hand to her crotch, not through her pocket this time but openly.

She looks around, all the time walking on the spot. You daren't move from the shadows.

She goes up to a tourist information board, stands there a moment while the drunks pass. She is standing away from you, as if pretending to read the board. The drunks disappear into the distance and Diane looks to see if they have gone, but doesn't move. She seems to be fiddling with her skirt, is bent slightly forward and then –

She is peeing. She must have a hand beneath the front of her dress holding her knickers aside because a stream of pee is cascading to the ground, splattering on the pavement. The puddle sneaks across the pavement and across the kerb.

(Gosh!)

Diane turns and comes back out of the bus shelter, tidying down her dress, and bending to wipe her legs a little. But in her summer dress she looks as elegant as ever. She walks off through the car park towards the taxi rank, leaving you looking forward to your next date with her.

(Your date may have ended early, but it's not been without its reward.)

YOU HAVE WON A CONSOLATION PRIZE!
