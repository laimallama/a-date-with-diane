Not Watching Diane and the Brunette

Diane and the brunette have gone together behind the camper van. What are you going to do?

(Be a gentleman and get some brownie points by not watching at all?)

You stand guard while Diane and the girl have their pee. You don't try to watch.

Beats me why you are playing this game, but that's your choice.

They take quite a while and you hear some whispering before they emerge. You then escort them politely back to the road. You and Diane go to the taxi rank.

DIANE: Bother.

She chats quietly with the brunette. Then –

DIANE: Actually, d'you mind if I go home with Debbie? We both live in the same street and we've got lots to talk about. Thank you very much for a lovely evening. I'll see you next week.

She whispers to you: 'Debbie had a little bit of an accident so she doesn't really want the others to know. So I'll accompany her home in the taxi.' She gives you a nice kiss – and is gone.

(You go home.)

GOODBYE! THE GAME IS OVER.
