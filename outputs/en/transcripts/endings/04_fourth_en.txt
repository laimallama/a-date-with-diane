Fourth Prize

You pass a few houses, and come to a church.

You want to stop in the shelter of a lych gate for a kiss, but Diane hurries you along.

You ask her why she's walking so fast.

DIANE: It's cold, isn't it? I'll be glad to get home.

She isn't talking. She keeps looking left and right. She looks at the bushes to your left.

She looks at the bushes to your right. She seems to hesitate.

YOU: What are you looking at?

DIANE: Nothing.

(She hurries you along.)

Diane has gone very quiet. You can tell that she's desperate for a pee.

She keeps looking at the bushes as if she'd like to dash behind them. But it's a bit awkward for her, with you there.

Maybe you could help? Suggest she pop behind a wall or something?

(Think about it.)

Diane is hurrying you along as best she can. She isn't speaking much.

What are you going to do to help her?

(Suggest she go behind the wall?)

YOU: Diane, why don't you just pop behind this wall. Nobody will see you. I'll keep a lookout.

She stops and considers for a moment. She is bent forward, her hands between her knees.

DIANE: Do you mind? What a thing to happen on a first date!

YOU: Yes. Better than wetting yourself, though.

DIANE: Wouldn't that be awful? It happened to a friend of mine. Come on – hold my bag. And look the other way, won't you?

(You promise.)

She is walking on the spot, looking nervously around.

She is already holding the hem of her dress as she hurries behind the low wall of the lych gate. She stops there, looking quickly round again as she hitches up her frock. She quickly pulls her knickers down.

Even before she has fully squatted she is peeing, a furious stream cascading onto the concrete. In the half light you can see the steam rising from the puddle forming at her feet. After a few seconds she squats lower into a full squat, looking serious as she looks ahead, at the puddle now running across the ground.

You cannot take your eyes off her. Gradually the stream lessens and a few last trickles run down. She gasps with relief. 'Phew. Gosh, I needed that.'

She pulls up her knickers, which are white, hitching her frock higher as she does so. Then she looks across at you.

(You pretend to be looking away.)

Diane grins at you as she takes her bag. Then she puts her arms around you.

'Phew!' she says. 'I didn't half need that. But you are a rotter – you said you wouldn't watch.'

But she is smiling at you.

(And she can sense how excited you are.)

CONGRATULATIONS!

You've taken Diane out on a date and managed to get her really desperate on the way home.

So desperate that she's had to take an emergency squat, which you were able to watch.

YOU HAVE WON 4TH PRIZE!
