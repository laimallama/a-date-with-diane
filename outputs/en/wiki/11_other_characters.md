# Other characters

Minor figures associated with Welbourne, grouped according to the primary articles to which they relate.

## Connected with Simon Hartley

### Helen Hartley

Mother of Simon and Daniel Hartley. Resident in Scarborough with a second husband; associated with a small guest house. Maintains telephone contact and sends money around examination periods.

### Philip Hartley

Father of Simon and Daniel Hartley. Resident in a flat in a neighbouring town. Limited visits; occasional practical advice about the house.

### Louise

Simon Hartley’s sixth-form girlfriend for a period of three weeks.

### Karen

Colleague of Simon Hartley with whom he had a relationship lasting most of one winter.

### Sophie Wren

School contemporary of Simon Hartley, associated in his memory with a field trip and a locked pavilion toilet. She later left the area.

## Connected with Diane Ellison

### Margaret Ellison

Mother of Diane Ellison. Resident at the Ellison house. Remains awake when Diane returns late and expresses concern about employment at Beck & Partners and about London.

### Alan Ellison

Father of Diane Ellison. Retired driver on the East Coast line. Spends time in the garden. Throughout Diane’s childhood he instructed her to use the toilet before leaving the house.

### Paul Ellison

Older brother of Diane Ellison. Resident in Leeds. Collected postage stamps briefly as a teenager.

### Gary Hill

Former boyfriend of Diane Ellison for about eight months. Associated with long car journeys and refusal to stop for toilet breaks.

### Neil Cooper

Former school-bus acquaintance of Diane Ellison. Employed at a local garage.

### Linda and Jenny

Friends of Diane Ellison. Linda took part in an episode during a hen weekend in the Netherlands involving a men’s urinal. Jenny appears in an account of a roadside vehicle breakdown involving Margaret Ellison’s car.

### Chess-teaching family friend

Adult acquaintance who taught Diane Ellison chess at about the age of fifteen. Associated in her memory with cider at lunch and an uncomfortable private-house bathroom situation. Not named in detail in these articles.

## Connected with Molly Yates

### June Yates

Mother of Molly Yates. Lives further from the centre than the Ellison family. Attended the opening night of *Outside Edge*.

## Connected with Bruno Palmer

### Lucia and Anthony Palmer

Parents of Bruno Palmer, resident in Guildford. Anthony Palmer taught mathematics. Lucia Palmer came from near Bologna and maintains family contact and food parcels.

## Connected with Robert Miles

### Eleanor Miles

Daughter of Robert Miles. Attends selected first nights. Otherwise maintains practical correspondence.

## Connected with Daniel Hartley

### Toby, Thomas, and Bella

College friends of Daniel Hartley, aged 18. Appear at some post-examination gatherings in the Hartley house.

## Connected with Chloe Hollis

### Sharon Hollis

Mother of Chloe Hollis. Maintains a tidy house, requires shoes to be removed at the door, and leaves the hall light on when Chloe returns late.

## Connected with Amanda Marlow

### Geoff and Patricia Marlow

Parents of Amanda Marlow. Resident on a newer Welbourne estate. Differ in emphasis between secure employment and support for art education.

## Connected with Debbie Croft

### Craig Flynn

Boyfriend of Debbie Croft (born 1980), aged 25. Casual building work, ownership of a car, and repeated delay of toilet access. See [Debbie Croft](10_debbie_croft.md).
