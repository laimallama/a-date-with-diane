# Daniel Hartley

**Daniel Hartley** (born February 1987) is the 18-year-old younger brother of Simon Hartley. He lives in the family semi-detached house in Welbourne and has recently finished examinations at Welbourne College. Results are expected in August.

## Early life and family

He is nine years younger than Simon and does not remember Helen and Philip Hartley living together in the house as a couple. Helen Hartley lives in Scarborough following remarriage and is associated with a small guest house. Philip Hartley lives in a flat in a neighbouring town and maintains limited contact. Day-to-day household expenses are largely met by Simon. Daniel is fond of his brother and also regards him as markedly older in habits. Simon’s stamp collecting is a recurrent joke; Simon’s BMW is an object of uncomplicated envy.

## Education

At Welbourne College he showed intermittent academic ability and inconsistent application until the examination period. He hopes to proceed to university, most likely in computing, with an additional interest in design that he does not always declare among friends.

A college technician previously arranged for him to observe a Waterfront Theatre matinee from a technical position. He retains an interest in stage scenery. His laptop contains unfinished set drawings among other files.

## Personal life

His main male friends include Toby and Thomas. From the spring preceding the examinations he has also spent time with Chloe Hollis. The relationship is informal. After the examinations he sometimes hosts or joins larger gatherings at the Hartley house, including Amanda Marlow and Bella among others.

On at least one evening a camera concealed in the bathroom transmits images to his laptop. On other evenings the house is used only for ordinary social purposes. Discovery of the camera by Simon Hartley precipitates a major argument.

## Residence

He occupies the rear bedroom of the Hartley house.
