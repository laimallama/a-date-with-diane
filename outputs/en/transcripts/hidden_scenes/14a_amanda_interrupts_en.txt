The Bathroom at Home – Amanda Walks In on You

You have to get the book. But you're passing the bathroom, which is empty. What will it be?

(You could do with a pee yourself, so now's your chance.)

You head into the bathroom, shut the door, unzip and let fly.

(You were pretty desperate yourself!)

But you're still in full flow when the door bursts open. It's Amanda!

She stops in the doorway. 'Oh! I'm sorry!' she exclaims, and retreats onto the landing, shutting the door.

But she's so disturbed you that you've lost control of your aim. You've splashed pee on the floor and down your trouser leg.
