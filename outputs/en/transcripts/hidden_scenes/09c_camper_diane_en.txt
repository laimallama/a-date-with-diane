Peeping Round the Back of the Camper Van

Diane and the brunette have gone together behind the camper van. What are you going to do?

(Peep round the back of the camper van?)

It's quite dark, so they probably won't see you if you peep at them. And how can you resist?

You watch them hurry behind the bus. The girl from the bus stop is in a real hurry, already hitching up her skirt. But Diane looks back at you, so you have to stay out of sight.

Once they are behind the bus, you tiptoe round to the far end of the minibus and peep. You can see the steam rising from the puddle forming on the ground.

Diane is finishing her pee, and your heart is beating as you watch her there, her neat little bottom just above the ground. Then she too stands, and you quickly dodge back behind the camper van.

(You wait for them.)

The girls re-emerge. They are whispering to each other. The brunette seems to be wiping her legs. You hear Diane say, 'Don't worry, it doesn't show.'

They join you again. They don't seem to know you were watching.

Diane takes your hand. The three of you walk back to the road. The brunette leaves you and heads for her bus stop.
