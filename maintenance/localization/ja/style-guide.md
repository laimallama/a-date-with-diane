- Clothing: ショーツ, ペチコート, ガーター, タイツ, ストッキング, ワンピース. Bare legs are 生脚, not bare feet. Brunette is 茶色い髪の女性 where that description matters.
- Currency: 100ポンド, 3ポンド; retain decimal points for fractional pounds and never convert to yen. Visual currency uses the same suffix.
- Preserve English play titles inside Japanese title marks, e.g. <em>『Outside Edge』</em>.
- Dialogue uses Japanese ： and 「」. Parenthesized delivery cues use <em>（…）</em>. Use ordinary Japanese clause punctuation and no word-spacing; a full-width space can separate sentences following ！ or ？.
- Use standard restrained speech, without invented dialect, honorifics or decorative particles. Preserve differences between speakers through meaning and register.
