The First Time You Saw Diane

You are very conscious of this pretty girl sitting next to you. The skirt is quite long, down to her knees, but you can imagine her legs beneath it.

You both watch the play.

It is a comedy set at a cricket match. The couples meet, and the captain's wife prepares the teatime picnic, and the couples argue, and so it goes on.

But you've seen it before, so your attention wanders.

What are you thinking about?

(Thinking about the way you met?)

Your mind has wandered and you are thinking back to the time when you first met.

You were at the bus stop on the way home, late in the evening, when you noticed Diane a few places ahead of you in the queue. What caught your attention was that she was clearly dying for a pee. She was fidgeting about, walking on the spot and as the minutes passed, she started pacing about, a hand deep in her trouser pocket. The only trouble was that you were desperate yourself and rather than stand and watch had to retreat into the car park for an urgent pee.

You were just finishing when you heard hurrying footsteps and it was Diane. She was already unbuckling her belt. You stepped behind a car and were able to watch her as she pulled her trousers down and squatted behind a minibus. She didn't half need to go! Afterwards you followed her back to the bus stop at a safe distance, only to find that the bus had left without the two of you. So you shared a taxi home, and chatted.

Of course, she doesn't know that you watched her having that pee!

(Concentrate on the play.)

The play has reached a very amusing scene. Everybody is laughing.

You look across at Diane, who is concentrating on the play.

What will you do?

(Just carry on watching the play.)

There is a slight pause for a scenery change.

You offer her the bottled water. She drinks almost half of it.

DIANE: Thank you. I was glad of that. It's quite hot in here.

YOU: Tell me if you want more.

DIANE: Thank you. That's sweet of you.

You gained 1 intimacy point.

(Carry on watching the play.)

The play continues. Diane laughs and so do you.

One couple likes another but hates another, some have social aspirations, and one of the women, feeling snubbed, has gone off to sulk in the car.

Meanwhile the men continue to play cricket.

Diane is shifting about a bit. Maybe she's just uncomfortable, or maybe she's beginning to need the loo.

(Carry on watching the play.)

The plot of the play is growing more complicated.

However, you've seen it before so you know what's going to happen. You look across at Diane, who is concentrating on the actors on stage.

What will you do?

(Just carry on watching the play.)

Your mind is wandering again.

You think back to the time when you first saw her and you'd secretly watched her having a pee. She'd been absolutely desperate – you could tell that by the way she was shifting about at the bus stop, constantly looking anxiously around.

Then when you saw her hurry into the car park, she was already unbuckling her belt and couldn't even stand still while she was unzipping her trousers. The moment she'd got them clear, even before she was fully squatting, she was peeing. And then, when she did squat down, the puddle just grew and grew.

You actually wonder whether she'd got her knickers down in time!

Maybe one day you'll be able to ask her.

(Concentrate on the play.)

Diane seems to sense that your mind is wandering.

You watch the play more intently. It really is very funny.

You wonder what's happened to the girl who's sulking in the car. You rather liked her. Ah yes – you do remember what happens!

You look at Diane and she looks at you. You both smile.

You gained 1 intimacy point.

DIANE: (whispers to you) The one playing the girl who's gone off sulking, that's Molly, my friend.

Diane keeps crossing her legs, first one way, then the other.
