Chloe Pees Beside Her House

Suddenly, as you walk back home, you hear footsteps hurrying towards you.

A girl comes into view on the other side of the road.

It's Chloe, your brother's girlfriend, and she's in a real hurry!

(You step into the shadows.)

You stay where you are, hardly daring to breathe, stooping low so that Chloe can't see you. Her house is only about 20 yards away, in a turning just off the road. There don't seem to be many lights on.

She hurries up to her house. She rings the bell, and stands there, hopping from foot to foot.

She turns away from the door, as if looking to see if anyone is around, then shoves a hand between her legs, really holding herself. She is walking on the spot in a little circle.

Wouldn't it be great if her mum didn't hear the doorbell!

Do you have any luckshots left? Maybe the doorbell's broken?

(Yes, I've got one left.)

Nobody is answering the doorbell. It must be out of order. Oh dear!

Chloe is pacing about, openly holding herself, clearly frantic to pee. Suddenly she sits down on a little bench and sits there, still holding herself, shifting urgently backwards and forwards. She gets up and rings the bell again, her free hand still at her crotch. She turns towards you and suddenly crosses her legs urgently, holds herself with both hands, and bends forward, as if perhaps a spurt has escaped. She is now holding herself all the time, so firmly that her tartan skirt is partly lifted up at the front.

Suddenly she moves to the side of the building, nearer to where you are standing. She looks around and holds the hem of her skirt as if she is about to have a squat pee.

She hears a car going up the side road, pushes her skirt back into place, and goes back to the door. She can hardly walk upright.

(Carry on.)

Chloe rings the bell again. Nobody answers.

She walks around in a little circle, one hand firmly at her crotch.

She rings the bell again. Nobody answers.

She sits down on the little bench, bouncing up and down.

She paces about. She moves again to the side of the building. She looks around, then starts to hitch up her skirt.

In your excitement you break a twig off the tree you're standing behind.

She turns and looks straight at you.

(Duck out of sight.)

She hasn't seen you. It's dark where you are, but you can see her in the lights outside the house.

She does a little knees up dance, then a curtsey and shoves both hands to her crotch. You imagine a squirt of pee dribbling down her leg.

She goes back to the door. She rings the bell again. Nobody answers. She crosses one leg tightly over the other.

She sits on the bench, hunched forward, bouncing up and down. Her hand looks as if it's right under her skirt.

She paces about, then walks, half runs to the side of the building, just over the road from where you are. She seems amazingly close.

(And then...)

Suddenly she stops in her tracks. She bends slightly forward. You hear her gasp, 'Oh no!'

She suddenly doubles up, a hand clutched frantically between her legs. She looks quickly around.

She hobbles a few feet, reaches under her tartan skirt and in one movement pulls her tights and panties halfway down her thighs. Her panties are purple.

Even before she is squatting, a stream of pee is pouring from her, splattering onto to the paving. She is scarcely in a half squat, but now pushes her tights further down.

She looks anxiously around, now squatting further down. You can no longer see her stream, but watch as the puddle runs away from her.

Eventually she is finished. She wipes her legs and you get the feeling that her panties are far from dry. But she pulls them on, and then her tights.

And finally her mum answers the door and a rather sheepish Chloe disappears into the house.

(A nice end to the evening, but—)

GOODBYE! THE GAME IS OVER.
