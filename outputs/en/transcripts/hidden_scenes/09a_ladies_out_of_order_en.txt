You have a pee. When you emerge from the Gents you find Diane is already there. You're quite surprised.

She is looking rueful.

DIANE: It's awful in there. It's been vandalised. I'll have to wait.

YOU: Oh dear. Is it urgent? Maybe you could use the Gents.

Diane considers. She is shifting from foot to foot.

DIANE: I do want to go. D'you think it will be all right?

YOU: I'll keep a lookout.

(You take her hand and lead her into the Gents.)

Diane goes into the Gents. You follow – she may not have expected that, but you are entitled to go there, after all, so she doesn't say anything.

Diane dashes into the cubicle and slams the door to. The door doesn't reach the ground and you can see her legs, walking on the spot even as she fumbles with her clothing. You just have to visualise her there. The rustling of her skirt and petticoat, followed by the loud hiss and splatter of her pee, leaves little to the imagination. There is an audible gasp of relief.

(You listen in wonder.)

You gained 25 intimacy points.

The toilet flushes. Diane comes out of the cubicle, adjusting her clothes. You step away from the door.

Diane washes her hands, then leaves the toilet. 'I'll see you outside,' she says.

You meet Diane outside. She gives you a rueful grin.

Diane takes your arm. She is really grateful for your help. You walk on to join the others, who are looking back from some distance away.
