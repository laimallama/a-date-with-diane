Diane Phones You the Next Morning

You just wish you'd been able to see how Diane got on. Wouldn't you like to ask her?

(Yes.)

You go home. The next morning you phone Diane to tell her how you enjoyed the date.

You chat about this and that. Then—

YOU: You rather dashed away at the end. I hope I hadn't upset you.

DIANE: (with a nervous little giggle) I'm sorry—I was absolutely desperate for the loo.

YOU: Did you make it?

DIANE: (with another giggle) Just about.

YOU: What d'you mean, "Just about"?

(She giggles again.)

DIANE: Yes, I'm sorry I had to dash. Honestly, I was bursting for a pee. I'd have stopped and said goodbye properly but, well, I couldn't wait.

YOU: Couldn't wait? Didn't you go in the back door?

DIANE: (giggles) Goodness no. I just couldn't wait. Lucky I was wearing a dress.

YOU: You mean you—

DIANE: I just stood and did it.

She pauses.

DIANE: I wet my knickers. It's no big deal, is it? It was dark, no one could see me, you couldn't see me. And I'd been wanting to go for ages. It's not the first time it's happened at the end of a night. As long as no one sees you there's no harm done. Anyway, I must dash. See you next week.
