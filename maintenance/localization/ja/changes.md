Direct translation from the frozen English catalog. Existing editions remain unchanged.

## Language decisions and revisions

- Contemporary plain narration, natural dialogue and polite Notes/status prose.
  No added dialect, honorifics or exaggerated character speech. Subjects are omitted
  only when the actor stays clear.
- British currency, measurements, names and setting retained. Pounds use ポンド;
  fractions retain decimal points. No yen conversion or invented measurements.
- Clothing, age, day, wine, food, earlier conversations and speaker knowledge were
  reviewed in the source packets with their predicates. Repeated English strings
  were reused only where context also matched.
- Generic toilet references use トイレ or お手洗い; gendered facilities are named
  when relevant. Bathroom use does not imply taking a bath or shower.
- Calibration refined the objective, clothing, bare legs, poster quantity,
  ten-minute duration and invitations. Later batches refined literal idioms,
  actor ambiguity, completed versus impending actions, and degrees of urgency.

## Integration and evidence

Japanese has standalone, English/Japanese, visual and bilingual visual games.
Japan-specific fonts and strict Japanese line breaking apply only while Japanese
is selected. Narrow status cells allow normal Japanese wrapping. English fonts,
existing palettes, Notes spacing and scrolling behavior are preserved.

`reviews.json` and `surface-reviews.json` record exact editorial coverage.
`../verification.md` records final technical checks and their limits. Automated
checks do not certify linguistic perfection. No independent native-speaker or
new browser-rendering review is claimed.
