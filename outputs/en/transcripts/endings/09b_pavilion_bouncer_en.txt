The Bouncer Lets Diane In

Diane has disappeared, urgently seeking a toilet. You want to see how she gets on.

Casting all discretion to the winds, and chancing your luck, you head off in search of her.

You leave the bus stop.

(Where did she go?)

Which way did she go? You look around and will have to guess. Which will it be?

(Back towards the Pavilion? Maybe she's hoping she'll be able to get in to use the Ladies.)

You head back towards the Pavilion. You look left, you look right. There's no sign of Diane.

You look across the car park. Maybe she's popped behind a car. But you can't see her, just a few stragglers heading for their cars.

You look across to the main entrance to the Pavilion. There is a bouncer at the door.

Suddenly you hear footsteps, the sound of a girl running.

It's Diane. You stand back into the shadows and watch as she goes up to the door of the Pavilion.

She goes up to the doorman. She asks him something.

Looks like you might need another luckshot. Can you play one?

(Yes, I've kept one back. I'll use it now!)

Diane is asking the bouncer if she can go in to use the Ladies, even though it's past closing time.

And luckily you still have a luckshot.

You play it. The bouncer shakes his head.

(Will Diane plead with him?)

Diane is pleading with the bouncer. You can see her shifting urgently from foot to foot.

But your luckshot has worked and the bouncer takes pity on her. Well, you did want to help her, didn't you?

So he lets her in to use the toilet. Isn't she lucky!

You'll just have to imagine her in there, hurrying into a cubicle, the struggle to get her knickers down in time...

(Time to go home.)

GOODBYE! THE GAME IS OVER.
