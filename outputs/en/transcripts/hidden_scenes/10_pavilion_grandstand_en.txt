(It is almost pitch black inside.)

Your eyes quickly grow accustomed to the half-light in the room.

Along one side of the room is a long window, high up in the wall. Beneath it is a row of wooden chairs.

A man is standing on one of the chairs. He waves a greeting to Robert.

The waiter beckons you to stand on one of the chairs. You follow Robert's example and do so, and look through the window.

You have a grandstand view of the Ladies lavatory!

(Gosh!)

Two or three girls are there, washing their hands or tidying their make-up, but you are too high up to have a good view of them.

But between them and the cubicles is the line of girls waiting their turn. And you have a perfect view.

There are about eight girls in the queue, with Diane and Molly over to your left.

But for the moment you are more concerned with a petite blonde, second in the queue, wearing a short denim skirt. She clearly can't stand still and keeps looking for a cubicle to become vacant.
