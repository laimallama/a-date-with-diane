# German (Germany)

Use standard contemporary German, with restrained, lightly literary narration and
natural dialogue. Preserve the British setting and understated humour. Avoid archaic
wording added merely to suggest age, regional dialect and invented explanations.

Narration addresses the player as `du`. Diane, friends and peers use `du`; service
encounters normally use `Sie`. Choose address by relationship, not source pronouns
alone. Player speaker label is `DU:`. Other names retain their spelling.

Use German comma rules and `„…“` / `‚…‘` quotation marks. Retain HTML structure and
semantic emphasis, with cues in italic parentheses. Use ordinary German apostrophe
rules. Choice instructions use natural imperatives; transitions can be concise.

Keep pounds sterling. In prose use amounts such as `12 £`, `3,50 £`; the balance and
HUD use decimal commas and retain their existing numerical values. English bilingual
layers keep English formatting. British food and wine remain British/European facts.
