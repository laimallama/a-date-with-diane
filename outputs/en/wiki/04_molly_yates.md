# Molly Yates

**Molly Yates** (born spring 1979) is a 25-year-old actress from Welbourne and the closest friend of Diane Ellison. She appears in a cameo role in *Outside Edge* at the Waterfront Theatre during the production’s final week.

## Early life and family

Yates attended the same comprehensive school as Diane Ellison and performed with her in the Welbourne Players. She was more persistent than Ellison about continuing with acting after school. She did not take a university degree. Her mother, June Yates, lives further from the town centre than the Ellison family, in a terrace house. Her father left when she was twelve; subsequent contact has been intermittent.

After school she worked in youth theatre, lived for a period in shared housing in Manchester, attended unpaid auditions, and appeared in at least one television commercial.

## Career

The Waterfront Theatre engagement is paid professional work in her home town, opposite Bruno Palmer. In *Outside Edge* she plays a character who leaves in anger and, later, a character unable to enter a clubhouse toilet. Robert Miles rehearsed the second sequence as a physical as well as comic problem, including asking Yates to arrive at an afternoon rehearsal with an unmet need to urinate. She cut her hair short for the role and retained the style.

She has no confirmed contract immediately after the production closes. Options include returning to Manchester, taking temporary non-acting work, and further regional auditions.

## Personal life

She travels to Italy with Diane Ellison in most years and usually organises their summer watersports. She missed Ellison’s hen weekend in the Netherlands because of rehearsals. She knows Ellison’s long-standing difficulty admitting an urgent need to urinate in company and does not treat the subject as comic material between them.

During the Welbourne run she leaves the theatre with Bruno Palmer after Tuesday and Saturday performances and with Robert Miles on Thursdays. She is fond of Palmer without describing the arrangement as an established couple. She respects Miles as a director and accepts his rehearsal methods as professional demands rather than personal eccentricity. After the final performance she does not know whether contact with Palmer will continue.

## Residence

She has no permanent base in London. Between contracts her life remains tied to Welbourne and to temporary accommodation.
