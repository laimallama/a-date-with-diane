Diane Turns the Tables

Isn't it great? Diane wants to go to the loo, but is delaying it, knowing that it turns you on.

What's it to be?

(Say you'd like her to wait a bit longer.)

YOU: I'd love it if you'd wait a bit longer.

DIANE: Well, a few minutes maybe.

You pour out some more wine. She drinks. You are both getting slightly tipsy, in a gentle way.

(She shifts about on the sofa.)

Diane is dying for a pee. The trouble is, you are bursting to go yourself.

YOU: Actually, I must go for a pee.

DIANE: (laughing) I won't let you!

She seizes you but you eventually break away and hurry upstairs to the bathroom. You certainly needed a pee.

When you come out, Diane is waiting outside the door. She gives you a grin and dashes into the bathroom, shutting the door before you can gather your senses.

And all you can do is listen to the urgent rustling of her clothing and then the loud sibilant tinkle of her pee.
